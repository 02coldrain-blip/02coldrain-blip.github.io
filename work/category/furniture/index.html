AI furniture videos and product images

We create furniture visuals for product presentations, advertising and social content. A sofa, chair or armchair can appear in a restrained interior or an imagined landscape, with attention to shape, texture and light.

What we can make

Still images, short animated scenes or an edited sequence. The portfolio includes furniture in nature, seating studies and a sofa-detail film.

What to send us

Clear product photos or approved references, material and colour information, the intended setting and the required format. Identify any dimensions, construction details or finishes that must not change.

What to keep in mind

AI can alter product proportions and fine details. Approve the visuals against your product references; generated images should not be treated as precise technical or catalogue documentation without review.

Budget, timing and deliverables

Each project is quoted individually. Scene count, complexity, editing, sound and format adaptations affect the scope. We agree the deliverables and timing after reviewing your brief; urgent production is available on request.
